I wanted a place to record my life as it happens: what I do for a living, what I enjoy, and how I feel.

Twenty years from now, I want to come back and read these pages. I want to remember the ordinary days as well as the milestones.

## What I want to keep

The things I am learning. My running experiences. Places I visit. Thoughts I might otherwise forget. How my priorities change over time.

## A note to future me

When you return to this entry, I hope the pages that follow help you remember the person you were and the life you were living.
