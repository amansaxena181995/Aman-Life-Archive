CHILDHOOD, LEARNING & FINDING MY WAY

My story, so far.

From the child I was to the network architect I became.

I grew up in Farrukhabad with my five sisters, my mother, father, and grandfather. We did not have much money, but I still remember the simple things from my childhood.

I used to play gully cricket, carrom, and fly kites. Most days were about going to school, playing a little, and then having a good sleep on the terrace. I did my schooling in Farrukhabad through the local state board.

My mother loved me a lot and was very protective. She never let me go anywhere alone until I left my hometown for college.

I did my B.Tech in Computer Science at Invertis University in Bareilly. I enjoyed those four years of hostel life. But honestly, I never ran a program in C, Java, or even made a page in HTML during college. 😄

For around 10–12 months, I also prepared for the civil services. Looking back, I think my wish to get selected was connected to someone in my life. When that person left, I also left the preparation. This happened towards the end of college.

In June 2016, after college, I came to Delhi NCR to find a job. I had no idea what kind of job I would get. I did not know how to communicate properly, and I did not know programming or much of the practical side of computer science.

Luckily, I joined a coaching institute for CCIE Routing and Switching training, which included CCNA, CCNP, and CCIE. At that time, I did not even know much about Cisco. I joined mainly because they promised 100% job placement.

I don’t know what clicked, but I started liking the course. I completed my CCNA Routing and Switching and got a job as a System Engineer with a Cisco reseller based in Bengaluru.

That journey from Delhi to Bengaluru was another memory—travelling in Rajdhani, Second AC. Lol, before that, I had never even travelled in Sleeper class!

After that, the journey kept going. I continued studying while working full-time and eventually cleared my CCIE lab exam. I got my magic number—57578—on 17 October. I can never forget that day or those sleepless nights spent preparing alongside my job.

Over the years, I have worked as a Network Engineer, Project Engineer, and Senior Network Engineer, and my journey has brought me into network architecture.

Now I have around 10 years of experience making a network path between source A and source B, and juggling between IP headers and MAC addresses. 😄

I came to Delhi without knowing what kind of job I would do. Somewhere along the way, networking became my career.
